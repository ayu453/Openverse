# Community Code of Conduct

OPENVERSE aims to be welcoming and harassment-free for people of every background, discipline, and experience level.

Expected: kindness, constructive feedback, respect, source attribution, consent, and privacy.

Unacceptable: harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without consent.

Moderators may take proportionate action to protect participants. Significant actions should include a reason and an appeal route where safe and appropriate.
