# OPENVERSE Governance — Discussion Draft

This is a proposal for discussion, not yet a binding constitution.

## Goals
Transparent decisions, meaningful participation, minority protections, and separation of product prioritization from moderation and security response.

## Proposal lifecycle
Draft → Discussion → Scheduled vote → Closed → Result published → Implementation tracking.

Each proposal must state the problem, exact decision, options, voter eligibility, voting period, quorum, decision rule, tie handling, implementation owner, and conflicts of interest.

## Safeguards
- One authenticated eligible account per proposal, enforced by a unique database constraint.
- No paid votes or transferable voting power.
- Individual ballots are not public by default.
- Rate limits and abuse review.
- Public results and auditable process events.
- Appeals for eligibility or process errors.

Moderators may take temporary, proportionate action for immediate safety, privacy, spam, or security risks. Actions should be documented and reviewed. A vote cannot authorize unlawful activity or exposure of private data.

A future ratification proposal must define constitutional amendments, membership eligibility, quorum, and independent appeal processes.
